PRIVACY POLICY — SIMON MUSICAL - EAR TRAINING

Last updated: [fecha de hoy]

This Privacy Policy describes how Simon Musical - Ear Training ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our application.

1. NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED

The App does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to play.

2. GAME PROGRESS STORAGE

Your progress (level records, Career Mode path, unlocked instruments, streak tree, game history) is stored locally on your device. Optionally, if you choose to connect the App with your Google Play Games profile, this progress may also be stored in your Google Play Games "Saved Games", associated with your Google account, so you can recover it on a new device. This is entirely optional and managed by your own Google Account settings.

3. ADVERTISING (GOOGLE ADMOB)

The App displays optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, which players can choose to watch in exchange for extra lives. AdMob may collect device identifiers and advertising data to serve and measure ads. You can learn more about how Google handles this data at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

4. GOOGLE PLAY GAMES SERVICES

If you sign in with Google Play Games, the App may access basic profile information (such as your display name and profile icon) and save your game progress ("Saved Games") to your Google account, so it can be restored if you reinstall the App or switch devices. This is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The App is designed to be family-friendly and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We recommend that parents supervise their children's use of the App and review the permissions and account settings on the device being used, including advertising settings.

6. DATA SHARING

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, other than the services described above (Google AdMob and Google Play Games Services), which operate under their own privacy policies.

7. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
